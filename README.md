Motion and 3D designer who builds his own tools.

Most of what's here started because a production needed it: an After Effects panel driven by a local language model, ComfyUI pipelines that run on my own workstation, and small utilities for the files that pile up around motion work. I design and direct the code through AI coding agents, and the judgment calls and the testing are mine.

**[ae-llama](https://github.com/crypticnull/ae-llama):** After Effects, driven by a local model through 70+ tools

**[voidfall](https://github.com/crypticnull/voidfall):** a roguelike in alpha, with art from my own ComfyUI workflows

**[metadata_stripper](https://github.com/crypticnull/metadata_stripper):** strips metadata from photos and video, originals untouched

**[h3_dance_studio](https://github.com/crypticnull/h3_dance_studio):** beat-locked pose control for AI video, still early
